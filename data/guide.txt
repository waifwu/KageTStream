You have now completed the main storyline. All that is left are the
"Ten Nights of Dream" which are short, self-contained stories, the various Summer
Festivals, and one more bonus, "Dreaming, Drinking Moon." These are "for fun" stories
and, of course, have no bearing on the main plot at all, and are questionably canonical.
Viewing of these stories are required to unlock some CGs.
